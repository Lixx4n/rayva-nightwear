A family business shaped by years of experience in the nightwear market.

RAYVA brings together a long-standing understanding of nightwear, customer preferences and the needs of wholesale buyers. Our focus is on offering a variety of styles for retailers and customers looking for night suits, fancy nightwear and bridal collections.

Over more than three decades in the market, relationships and experience have helped shape the business. We continue to focus on product variety, practical value and helping buyers find styles suited to their customers.
